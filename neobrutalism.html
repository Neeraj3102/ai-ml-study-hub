What Makes This "Neo-Brutalism"?

The 4 core engineering rules of this design movement

1. Hard Offset Shadows No blurry, soft Gaussian shadows. Everything uses high-contrast unblurred black offsets: 4px 4px 0px #000.
2. Chunky 3px Borders Every card, button, tag, and modal is bounded by bold, unapologetic solid black lines that enforce absolute visual separation.
3. High-Voltage Colors Vibrant neon pop highlights (electric yellow, hot magenta, acid lime, cyan) set against warm off-white or cream paper backgrounds.
4. Mechanical Feedback Buttons translate down and right when pressed (`active:translate(3px, 3px)`), physically collapsing their drop shadow like a real arcade switch.
